✈️ Visas & Airport Arrival

US passport holders require an E-Visa prior to entry.

  • Where to Apply: Directly on the official Vietnam Immigration Portal.
  • Cost: $25 USD (single entry).
  • Timeline: Processing takes ~3–5 business days, but we recommend applying 3–4 weeks before departure.

Immigration lines at Hanoi (HAN) Airport can be long during peak arrival hours. You can pre-book an optional VIP Fast Track Arrival Service (via travel platforms like Klook or Viator for ~$25 USD). An agent will meet you at your gate with a sign and guide you through priority immigration lanes. ✨

When flying out of US airports, ensure your TSA PreCheck or Global Entry Known Traveler Number (KTN) is linked to your airline ticket reservation for expedited US airport security.

🚕 Transportation & Internet

While we are not arranging direct shuttle buses, getting into town is very easy:

  • Rideshare: Uber does not operate in Vietnam. Download Grab (Southeast Asia's Uber equivalent) before you land and set up your US credit card. 📱
  • Travel Times: Airport to Old Quarter (Pho Co/Hoan Kiem) is ~40–50 mins. Airport to Cau Giay area is ~30–40 mins.
Shared Rides 🚘: Closer to February, we will gather arrival times to help connect guests landing at similar times to ride together!
  • Airport Wi-Fi: Free Wi-Fi is available in the airport terminal upon arrival.
  • eSIM (Recommended): Purchase a digital eSIM via apps like Airalo, Holafly, or Maya Mobile prior to flying for instant 4G/5G data upon landing. 📲
  • Physical SIM Card: Local SIM cards (Viettel or Vinaphone) can be purchased at airport kiosks after customs for ~$10–$15 USD.

  • Cash (Vietnamese Dong - VND): Recommended for street food, traditional markets, and small purchases. You can exchange USD at airport kiosks or draw cash at local ATMs using debit cards. 🪙
  • Credit Cards: Major hotels, sit-down restaurants, and shops accept Visa and Mastercard. 💳

🌸 Weather & Local Recommendations

February brings late winter / early spring weather to Hanoi. Expect cool temperatures between 59°F and 70°F (15°C – 21°C). It can occasionally be misty or chilly in the evenings, so pack light jacket layers and an umbrella! 🧥☔

  • Sightseeing: Stroll around Hoan Kiem Lake, visit Ngoc Son Temple, the Temple of Literature, and Hoa Lo Prison. 📸
  • Food & Drink: Enjoy authentic hot Phở, sample Bún Chả (grilled pork with noodles), and sip traditional Egg Coffee at Café Giảng. ☕🍲
